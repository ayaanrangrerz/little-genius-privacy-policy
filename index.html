Privacy Policy – Little Genius

Effective Date: 17 November 2026

Little Genius is an educational app designed for children. We respect children's privacy and are committed to protecting their information.

1. Personal Information

Little Genius does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, age, email address, photographs, or phone number.

2. Account and Login

The app does not require users to create an account or log in.

3. Learning Progress

Learning progress is stored locally on the user's device and is not uploaded to our servers.

4. Advertising

Little Genius does not display advertisements.

5. Purchases

The app does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

6. Analytics

The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, according to the developer's current information.

7. Third-Party Websites

The app does not provide links to external websites.

8. Children's Privacy

Little Genius is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updates will be published on this page.
